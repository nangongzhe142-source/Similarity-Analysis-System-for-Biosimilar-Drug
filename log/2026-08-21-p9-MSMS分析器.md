```

  comet_runner.py    # Comet 定位、params、ASCII 暂存、子进程搜库

  fdr.py             # target-decoy FDR 过滤

app/adapters/msms_sequence.py
```

Registry：`msms-sequence` → `MsmsSequenceAdapter`。
